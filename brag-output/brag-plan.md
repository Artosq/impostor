# /brag — IMPOSTOR

**What:** A party game you play in the browser. Everyone gets the secret word, except the impostor, who only gets a hint and has to bluff.
**For:** Groups of friends in one room, each person using their own phone.
**Hook:** Four role cards flip one by one. Three show the word "TO" in green; the fourth flips red: IMPOSTOR, with the hint "HORROR".
**Angle:** "Wszyscy znają hasło. Oprócz jednego." The whole game fits in that one sentence.
**Tone:** default, with a touch of suspense. 120 BPM, minor key, a hit when the impostor is revealed.
**Identity:** Silkscreen pixel font, #161616 background with the app's 70px grid, neon --danger #e74c3c and --secondary #2ecc71, logo4.png.
**Material:** Real screenshots from a live 4-player game run on the local server (Playwright). The nicknames Kasia/Tomek/Ola/Maciek are made up, and the "Nasza ekipa" category was typed in during the capture.
**Highlights:** (1) a room plus friends joining, (2) 1,300+ words (589+234+254+129+106) plus your own category, (3) voting, then the result screen "WYGRANA – Impostor został złapany".
